# Student and trainer guide

## For students

The Reading Tracker helps you build a reading habit with the **printed books** you borrow from
the school library. It records when and how long you read, the pages you read and what you learned.

### Sign in

Use the username, email or student ID and the password your school gave you. The first time,
you will be asked to choose a new password (at least 10 characters with letters and numbers).

If your school has opened self-registration, the sign-in page shows **Create an account**:
enter your full name, student ID, class, an optional email and a password. You are signed in
right away and sign in later with your student ID.

### Add a book

1. Open **Books** → **Add book**.
2. Search by title, author, ISBN or the library book ID and press **Add**.
3. Not in the list? Choose **Can’t find it? Add a new book** and type the details printed on the
   book. The librarian will check new entries; you can use the book immediately.

### Read with the timer

1. Tap **Read** (or **Start reading** on the dashboard or a book page).
2. Choose the book and tap **Start reading**. Open your book and read.
3. Taking a break? Tap **Pause**, then **Resume**. Paused time does not count.
4. When you stop, tap **Finish**, enter the page you stopped on (optional) and save.
   Tick **I finished this book** when you reach the end.

The timer keeps running on the server — you can close the page or lock your phone. If you
started it by mistake, use **Discard session**. Very long sessions (default 4 hours) are capped,
so remember to finish.

**Forgot the timer?** In **History** choose **Add session** and enter the date, start and end time.
Manual sessions are marked “Manual” and can be added for the last 7 days (school setting).

### Reflect on your reading

After finishing a session, choose **Write a reflection** (or **Reflect** in History). Answer the
questions you can — every field is optional — and rate how well you understood the text (1–5).
You can write in Khmer or English and edit a reflection later. In **Reflections** you can
search your notes, filter by book or dates, and tap a tag to see all reflections with it.
**Download PDF** saves a nicely formatted copy (one reflection, or all reflections matching your
filters) that you can keep or print. Your trainer may add feedback,
which appears under the reflection.

### Goals, streaks and analytics

- **Dashboard** shows today, this week and this month, your streak and goal progress.
- A **reading day** needs at least 10 minutes of finished reading (school setting). Your
  **streak** is the number of reading days in a row; it continues until today ends.
- In **Analytics → My reading goal** set your own minutes per day/week, reading days per week
  and books per month. Choose a steady goal — regular reading matters more than long sessions.

### Privacy

Only you, your assigned trainers and administrators can see your reading and reflections. No
rankings are shown to other students.

## For trainers

- **Overview** shows total reading time, average consistency, reflections waiting for feedback
  and students who **may need encouragement** (no reading for 7 days, school setting).
  Filter by class, generation and dates.
- **Students** is the list of all your students. Each row (a card on phones) shows the
  **current book** with a progress bar, **reading time** in the period with progress toward the
  student's weekly goal, **reading days**, **streak**, **last read**, and how many reflections
  are **to review**. Use search, class/generation filters and dates, or tick
  "Only students who may need encouragement".
- Open a student to see four tabs:
  - **Overview** — this week/month, streak, 7-day chart, books in progress, latest reflections.
  - **Reflections** — every reflection in full (what they read, learned, key ideas, how they will
    apply it, understanding rating, tags) with a feedback box under each one; filter
    "Needs feedback".
  - **Sessions** — every reading session with times, duration, pages, manual/capped markers.
  - **Books** — all books with status and page progress.

  Use **Export reading history** for a CSV of the student's sessions, and **Download all (PDF)**
  on the Reflections tab (or **PDF** on a single reflection) for a formatted reflection report.

- **Reflections** (menu) is your review queue across all students: reflections are shown in
  full, newest first, with an inline feedback box. After you send feedback the reflection leaves
  the "Needs feedback" list; switch to "All reflections" to see everything. Filter by class or
  search the text. You can edit your own feedback later ("Edit my feedback").
- **Reports** previews and downloads CSV reports for your students (summary, daily activity,
  weekly/monthly summary, encouragement list, reflection counts, completed books). Reflection
  text is never exported.
- Trainers can read but never change students' reading records.
